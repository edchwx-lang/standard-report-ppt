# V6.2.3 Post-Blueprint Deconstruction Contract

Scope is limited to the saved deconstruction PPTX after formal blueprint lock.
Pre-blueprint processing and bitmap mode are invariant.

The saved deck must exactly retain the company-template slide size,
master/layout inventory, and seed layout. Every slide must expose the five
editable skeleton objects, with the standard core-judgment typography and black
1 pt dashed border. Slides, masters, and layouts may not contain active shadow,
reflection, glow, soft-edge, 3-D, or nonzero inherited effect references.

The output audit emits `D623_TEMPLATE_MISMATCH`,
`D623_SKELETON_MISMATCH`, or `D623_FORBIDDEN_EFFECT`. Its report and the PPTX
are hash-bound to deconstruction acceptance and cached reuse.
